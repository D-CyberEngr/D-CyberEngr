## 👋 About Me

I'm **Miracle Egbedi**, known professionally as **D_CyberEngr**, an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate and engineering professional building at the intersection of engineering and cybersecurity.

My professional interests span **Control and Instrumentation, Industrial Automation, Cybersecurity, SOC Operations, Network Defense, and OT/ICS Security**.

With an engineering background and exposure to industrial project activities, I'm developing the cybersecurity knowledge and practical skills needed to understand, defend, and help secure both digital environments and industrial infrastructure.

My long-term goal is to become an **OT/ICS Security Engineer**, connecting engineering principles with cybersecurity practices to support the security, reliability, and resilience of industrial control systems.

I believe protecting industrial infrastructure begins with understanding how it works.

## ⚙️ Engineering Background

My engineering experience provides practical context for the industrial systems I aim to help secure.

### SCADA Revamp Project | Engineering Internship

During my internship, I supported activities during the **decommissioning and pre-commissioning stages of a SCADA revamp project**.

My exposure included:

- Supporting work involving primary and secondary cables connected to transmitters and distribution boards (DBs).
- Assisting with the installation and routing of two-core cables in 32 mm cable installations.
- Supporting cable glanding at transmitter and distribution board terminations.
- Assisting with cable tray installation and cable routing.
- Participating in insulation resistance and continuity testing.
- Supporting other installation and pre-commissioning activities as assigned.

This experience strengthened my understanding of field installation practices, instrumentation connections, cable management, and electrical testing. It also deepened my interest in the relationship between industrial operations, system reliability, and cybersecurity.

### Additional Engineering Exposure

- Electrical wiring and installation.
- Earth resistance testing.
- Maintenance of electrical components.
- Ongoing learning in industrial instrumentation.

## 🔐 My Security Philosophy

I see cybersecurity as the responsibility of protecting systems, information, and the operations that depend on them.

- **Confidentiality:** Protecting information from unauthorized access.
- **Integrity:** Maintaining the accuracy and trustworthiness of information and systems.
- **Availability:** Ensuring systems and services remain accessible when needed.

In industrial environments, these principles must be considered alongside operational safety, reliability, and continuity.

My ambition is to apply this understanding across digital and industrial environments, helping build systems that are secure, resilient, and dependable.
