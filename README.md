## 👋 About Me

I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduate with an interest in the intersection of **engineering, industrial automation, and cybersecurity**.

My engineering background has exposed me to electrical installations, maintenance activities, and instrumentation-related work. During my internship, I supported decommissioning and pre-commissioning activities associated with a SCADA revamp project, gaining practical exposure to field instrumentation, cabling, and testing procedures.

Alongside engineering, I'm developing my cybersecurity capabilities through structured training, hands-on labs, and continuous learning. My current focus is on **Security Operations Center (SOC) fundamentals and network defense**, while building the technical foundation needed to progress toward **Operational Technology (OT) and Industrial Control Systems (ICS) security**.

I believe securing industrial environments requires an understanding of both the digital systems that communicate and the physical processes they support.

## ⚡ Engineering Background

- Electrical wiring, installation, and maintenance activities.
- Exposure to SCADA revamp project activities during decommissioning and pre-commissioning.
- Support for primary and secondary cable installation, including cable routing and glanding at transmitters and distribution boards.
- Exposure to transmitter installations, distribution boards, cable trays, insulation resistance testing, and continuity testing.
- Developing knowledge of industrial instrumentation, control systems, and automation.

My engineering experience provides context for understanding how field devices, electrical infrastructure, and industrial control environments operate.

## 🛡️ Cybersecurity Focus

### Current Areas of Focus

- **SOC Operations:** Security monitoring, log analysis, event investigation, and incident response fundamentals.
- **Network Defense:** Network traffic analysis, security concepts, and understanding suspicious network activity.
- **Linux Security:** Working with Linux environments and common command-line tools.
- **Security Information and Event Management (SIEM):** Learning how security events are collected, monitored, and investigated using tools such as Wazuh.
- **OT/ICS Cybersecurity:** Building toward the security of industrial networks, SCADA systems, control systems, and connected operational environments.
- **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC):** Developing an understanding of security risk, controls, and compliance principles.

## 🔐 My Security Philosophy

I see cybersecurity as more than protecting computers and networks. In industrial environments, security also intersects with availability, safety, reliability, and the continuity of physical operations.

My approach is built around three core principles:

- **Confidentiality:** Protecting information from unauthorized access.
- **Integrity:** Preserving the accuracy and trustworthiness of information and systems.
- **Availability:** Ensuring systems and services remain accessible when needed.

Understanding these principles alongside engineering fundamentals is part of the foundation I'm building for a future in industrial cybersecurity.
