## Introduction

I'm Miracle Egbedi, an Electrical and Electronics Engineering graduate and engineering professional interested in Control and Instrumentation and Cybersecurity. My current cybersecurity focus is SOC Operations and Network Defense, with a long-term goal of becoming an OT/ICS Security Engineer.

I aim to apply engineering principles and cybersecurity knowledge to help protect digital systems and industrial infrastructure.

## About Me

My background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering has shaped my interest in industrial systems, instrumentation, operational reliability, and the technologies that support critical infrastructure. Alongside my engineering experience, I am developing my cybersecurity capabilities, with a focus on security monitoring, network defense, and industrial control system security.

During my engineering internship, I supported decommissioning and pre-commissioning activities on a SCADA revamp project. This gave me practical exposure to cable installation, cable glanding, cable tray installation, and electrical testing.

I have completed the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and undertaken additional cybersecurity training through TS Academy and Cisco Networking Academy. Through home labs and technical exercises, I continue to build my understanding of security monitoring, network defense, and security investigations.

My long-term goal is to become an OT/ICS Security Engineer, bringing engineering knowledge and cybersecurity skills together to help strengthen the security, resilience, and reliability of industrial control systems and critical infrastructure.

## Engineering Background

### SCADA Revamp Project | Engineering Internship

During my internship, I supported work during the decommissioning and pre-commissioning stages of a SCADA revamp project.

My tasks and exposure included:

- Working with primary and secondary cables associated with transmitters and distribution boards.
- Assisting with the installation of two-core cables in 32 mm cable runs.
- Supporting cable glanding at transmitters and distribution boards.
- Assisting with cable tray installation and cable routing.
- Participating in insulation resistance and continuity testing.
- Supporting other installation and pre-commissioning activities as assigned.

This experience strengthened my understanding of industrial installation practices, field instrumentation, electrical testing, and the importance of verification before systems are commissioned. It also strengthened my interest in industrial instrumentation, system reliability, and OT/ICS cybersecurity.

### Other Engineering Experience

My other engineering experience includes electrical wiring and installation, earth resistance testing, and maintenance of electrical components. I am also continuing to learn about industrial instrumentation.

## My Security Philosophy

I approach cybersecurity through the principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability, while recognizing the additional operational requirements that apply to industrial environments.

- **Confidentiality:** Safeguarding information against unauthorized access and disclosure.
- **Integrity:** Preserving the accuracy, consistency, and trustworthiness of information and systems.
- **Availability:** Maintaining reliable access to systems, services, and information when required.

Within OT/ICS environments, these principles must be considered alongside operational safety, reliability, and continuity. My long-term objective is to apply this understanding to the protection of connected industrial systems and critical infrastructure.
